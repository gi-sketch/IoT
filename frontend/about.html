Intelligent air quality monitoring for healthier and more sustainable campuses.

SMART ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

Building healthier campuses through data and intelligent monitoring.

AirIQ is an IoT-based Smart Air Quality Monitoring System designed to monitor campus air quality in real time. The system combines ESP32 sensor nodes, MQTT communication, machine learning, and a live web dashboard to detect pollution, predict future AQI, and assess health risks.
